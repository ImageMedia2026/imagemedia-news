Privacy information

A concise explanation of the information used to operate and improve IM News.

Audience measurement

IM News uses Google Analytics only after a visitor accepts analytics. It helps us understand visits, popular stories, reading behaviour and useful actions such as sharing an article. Advertising storage, Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled.

If analytics is declined, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. Your choice is saved in your browser and can be changed using the Privacy settings button.

Newsletter

When you subscribe, the email address you enter is stored in an Image Media-controlled Google Forms response spreadsheet and used for IM News email updates. It is not requested for ordinary website reading or audience measurement.

Your choices

You can decline analytics without losing access to any IM News article. To withdraw a newsletter subscription or ask a privacy question, contact contact@imagemedia.one.
