Leixões and Benjamin Kanuric Agree to Contract Termination

The Austrian midfielder leaves Estádio do Mar by mutual agreement, closing his spell at the club with 16 official appearances, three goals and two assists.

By Image Media News Staff — Matosinhos
or paste an image URL
Photo: Eduardo Castro

Leixões SC confirmed on July 30 that it has reached a friendly agreement with Benjamin Kanuric to terminate his contract, closing the Austrian midfielder's spell at Estádio do Mar just over a year after he arrived at the club.

Kanuric, 22, joined Leixões in July 2025 from Ingolstadt in Germany's third tier, having come through Rapid Vienna's academy before a spell in the German lower leagues that also took in Arminia Bielefeld. He is a former Austria youth international, having featured at the 2022 U19 European Championship.

16 official appearances · 3 goals · 2 assists

Across the 2025/26 season the midfielder made 16 official appearances for the Liga Portugal 2 side, scoring three goals and adding two assists, without fully establishing himself in a competitive Leixões midfield.

Image Media covered Leixões throughout last season, with photographer Eduardo Castro capturing Kanuric in action at Estádio do Mar across the campaign.
