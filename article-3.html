Stadium Tour Adds Second Night After Tickets Sell Out

Demand forces promoters to open an extra date at the same venue within hours of the first sellout.

By Priya Nair — Updated 1 hour ago — 3 min read
or paste an image URL

Tickets for the tour's first stadium date vanished within twenty minutes of going on sale, crashing the box office site's queue system twice in the process. By early afternoon, promoters confirmed what fans had been asking for all morning: a second night, at the same venue, the following evening.

The venue, which more usually hosts league matches on weekends, has staged only a handful of concerts in the past decade — most recently a one-off charity show that drew a fraction of the crowd expected this time. Organizers say the pitch will be fully protected, with load-in and stage construction beginning within hours of the final whistle of the previous match.

"We've never seen demand move this fast for a single date — adding the second night was the easy part."

Local transport authorities have already flagged the two dates for extended service, anticipating a similar footprint to a major cup fixture. Resale platforms are reporting listings well above face value, prompting the promoter to remind fans to buy only through official channels.

A third date has not been ruled out, though the tight turnaround before the venue's next scheduled fixture makes it unlikely. For now, fans are being told to expect an announcement on remaining ticket allocations later this week.
