KIDSROX Brings Youth Fitness Racing to Dublin

Young athletes took on an age-group fitness challenge built around movement, effort and a lively family atmosphere.

By IM News Editorial Desk · 31 August 2026 · Dublin, Ireland
or paste an image URL
A young athlete works through the sled station at Elite Fitness KIDSROX in Dublin. Photo: Jéssica Freitas / Image Media.

Elite Fitness KIDSROX brought young athletes and their families together in Dublin on Sunday, 30 August, for a day of age-group fitness racing.

The event placed the focus on movement and effort, with competitors progressing through a course that combined running with functional challenges. At the sled station, athletes had to keep driving forward while controlling a weighted load between the course markers.

30 August 2026 · Dublin, Ireland · Youth functional fitness · Organised by Elite Fitness

A competition designed around young athletes

KIDSROX adapted the language of fitness racing for younger competitors. The result was a structured sporting challenge, but also an event in which families could follow the action closely and celebrate each finish.

Podium presentations gave the age groups their own moment at the end of the competition. Away from the racing lanes, the event mascot met children and helped sustain the relaxed atmosphere around the course.

Event details

The official Monster Timing event record confirms the 30 August date and Dublin location, while the event’s race result listing identifies Elite Fitness as organiser.
